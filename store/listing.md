## Description
CrossRun is two cozy puzzle games on one wooden table.

CROSSWORD RUN
You start with two minutes. Finish a crossword and you win time back, more if it was clean and quick. The clock drains faster the longer you last, so every run ends eventually. The question is how far you get.
- Small 4x4 and 5x5 grids that take a minute, not an afternoon
- Every grid is freshly built, so no two runs are alike
- Streaks: solve words back to back for up to five times the points
- Give up on a word and keep moving, or spend points to reveal a letter
- A daily run with the same puzzles for everyone
- Every run ends with the full solution, so you always learn the word you missed

CARD RUN
No clock, just a well and a deck. Cards fall in pairs; tap a column to drop them, rotate to fit.
- Three of a kind in a line clears
- A run of three or more in one suit clears
- Fill a row with a pair or better for a poker hand
- Clears drop the cards above, and anything that forms as they land chains for more
- The pace rises as you score, and the run ends when the well overflows

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
- Thousands of clues, every one checked to be fair
- Wooden boards, paper cards, lanterns, and a sky that goes from day to dusk to night
- A soft soundtrack you can switch off
- Works completely offline, no account, no ads, nothing to buy inside

Your best runs and coins stay on your phone. Spend coins on new wood finishes, boosts, and golden letters.
