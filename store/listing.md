## Description
CrossRun is two puzzle games you play against one clock.

You start with two minutes. Finish a crossword, or clear a poker hand, and you win time back. The clock drains faster the longer you last, so every run ends eventually. The question is how far you get.

CROSSWORD RUN
- Small 4x4 and 5x5 grids that take a minute, not an afternoon
- Streaks: solve words back to back for up to five times the points
- Give up on a word and keep moving, or spend points to reveal a letter
- A daily run with the same puzzles for everyone
- Every run ends with the full solution, so you always learn the word you missed

CARD RUN
- Cards fall from a real 52-card deck into a five-wide well
- Tap a column to drop; any full row is a poker hand, pair or better clears it
- Stack five in a column for two pair or better and it clears too
- Clears drop the cards above, and a second hand chains for double points
- A high-card row stays as dead weight until you clear a strong hand on top of it

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
- Thousands of hand-checked clues across four difficulty tiers
- Warm wooden boards, paper cards, and a sun that goes down when you do
- Works completely offline, no account, no ads, nothing to buy inside

Your best runs, best score, and coins stay on your phone. Spend coins on new wood finishes for the board.
